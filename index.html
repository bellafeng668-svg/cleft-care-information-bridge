CARE PATHWAY

Cleft care is usually a sequence, not a single operation.

The exact plan depends on the patient's condition, age, development, and specialist assessment.

01Assessment

Start with an appropriate specialist assessment and understand individual needs.

02Primary treatment

Surgical planning and early interventions are determined by the clinical team.

03Follow-up

Healing, feeding, hearing, speech, dental development and other needs may require follow-up.

04Long-term care

Depending on the patient, later care may involve orthodontics, alveolar bone grafting, speech treatment, orthognathic surgery or psychological support.

Framework grounded in the National Health Commission's 2022 cleft-lip and cleft-palate guidelines and official specialist-hospital materials. Informational only; not medical advice.

Methodology

Each record includes source date, official source URL, evidence note, scope, and last-verified date. The research framework evaluates information friction through Findability, Completeness, Trustability, and Actionability. This is a starter dataset, not an exhaustive national directory.

ABOUT THE RESEARCH

Built around information friction.

This project asks a practical question: when care exists, how much harder is it to access because information is scattered, difficult to find, incomplete, or hard to act on?

FindabilityCan a family locate the relevant information?
CompletenessDoes the source answer practical questions?
TrustabilityCan the information be traced to a responsible institution?
ActionabilityDoes it give a useful next step?

Next research phase: expand systematically by province and city, then score and audit the four dimensions using a reproducible coding protocol.

This site does not diagnose, prescribe, or replace professional medical advice. Local and historical policies are labeled rather than presented as nationwide rules.
