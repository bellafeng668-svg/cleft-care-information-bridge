为什么做这件事WHY THIS MATTERS

信息应该成为桥梁，而不是障碍。Information should be a bridge, not a barrier.

唇腭裂相关的医疗信息并非不存在，而是分散在医院、基金会、政府部门与地方政策页面中。这个项目尝试把这些公开来源整理成更容易寻找、理解和核实的信息。Cleft-care information exists, but it is often scattered across hospitals, foundations, government agencies, and local policy pages. This project organizes those public sources into information that is easier to find, understand, and verify.

让信息更容易被找到，也让下一步更容易被看见。Make information easier to find—and the next step easier to see.唇腭裂医疗信息桥梁Cleft Care Information Bridge
