这是一个研究数据集，不是全国完整目录，也不对医院或资源进行排名。每条记录都保留官方来源链接，方便继续核实。This is a research dataset, not an exhaustive national directory or a ranking of hospitals or resources. Every record keeps its official source for verification.

统一编码CODING PROTOCOL

同一套规则整理每条来源One coding protocol across the dataset

每条记录按四个维度进行 0–2 分编码：可发现性、完整性、可靠性、可行动性。2 分表示来源直接、信息较完整且便于下一步使用；1 分表示部分满足；0 分表示该维度无法从公开来源确认。评分用于研究整理，不代表医院、基金或政策的质量排名。Each record is coded from 0–2 on four dimensions: findability, completeness, reliability, and actionability. A score of 2 indicates strong direct evidence and practical detail; 1 indicates partial evidence; 0 indicates that the dimension could not be established from the public source. Scores are for research organization, not quality rankings.
