信息摩擦：医疗资源为什么仍然难以找到？Information friction: why can care still be hard to find?

项目把唇腭裂医疗信息的“可获得性”拆解成可观察、可记录的维度，并用公开来源建立可追溯的数据基础。The project treats access to cleft-care information as an observable problem, breaking it into measurable dimensions and building a traceable public-source dataset.

可发现性Findability

家庭能否在合理的搜索路径中找到相关服务、机构和申请入口。Whether families can find relevant services, institutions and application routes through a reasonable search path.

完整性Completeness

关键的治疗、阶段、费用或支持信息是否足够完整。Whether key treatment, stage, cost or support information is sufficiently complete.

可靠性Reliability

信息是否能够追溯到医院、政府或公益组织等可靠来源。Whether information can be traced to a reliable hospital, government or charitable source.

可行动性Actionability

信息是否能帮助用户采取下一步行动，例如预约、咨询或申请救助。Whether information helps users take the next step, such as booking, contacting or applying for support.

数据如何形成？How the dataset is built

从国家指南、医院页面、基金会规则和地方政府公开信息中提取记录，保留原始来源、日期、服务类型、就诊阶段、支持方式和覆盖范围。Records are drawn from national guidance, hospital pages, foundation rules and local government information, preserving the original source, date, service type, care stage, assistance and scope.

外部研究如何支持这个问题？How does the literature frame the problem?

既有研究把信息障碍视为唇腭裂照护可及性的一部分：系统综述记录了治疗信息误传，以及交通、距离和经济等障碍；一项中国唇腭裂患者调查发现，搜索引擎和社交媒体是重要的信息来源，并指出信息应以患者能够理解和获取的方式呈现。2026年的中国医疗可及性研究也表明，数字素养与机构治理会影响居民对医疗可及性的感受。这里的项目数据因此聚焦于一个更窄的问题：公开信息本身是否足够可发现、完整、可追溯、可行动。Existing research treats information barriers as part of cleft-care access: a systematic review identified misinformation alongside transport, distance and financial barriers; a Chinese cleft-care survey found search engines and social platforms to be important information sources and emphasized communicating medical information in accessible ways. A 2026 study of healthcare access in China also linked digital literacy and governance to perceived accessibility. This project therefore focuses on a narrower question: whether public information itself is findable, complete, traceable and actionable.

  • Survey of medical-access information sources among cleft patients in China
  • 2026 study of perceived and spatial healthcare access in China

数据边界Dataset boundaries

本轮扩展至 50 条来源可追溯记录，覆盖国家指南、医院与高校专科来源、公益项目及地方医保/医疗救助信息。部分地方活动具有明确日期和适用范围，因此页面会标注“地方项目”或“活动项目”，避免把一次性活动误解为长期政策。This round expands the dataset to 50 source-traceable records covering national guidance, hospital and university specialist sources, charitable programs, and local insurance or assistance information. Time-bound local events are labeled as local programs or events so they are not mistaken for permanent national policy.
