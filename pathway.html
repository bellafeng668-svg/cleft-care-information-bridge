为什么需要就诊路径？Why a pathway?

家庭可能知道“有医疗资源”，却不知道什么信息应该先看。按照决策阶段重新组织分散的公开信息，可以减少这种信息摩擦。Families may know that care exists without knowing which information matters first. Reordering scattered public information by decision stage can reduce information friction.

始终核实最新信息Always verify current details

医院服务、预约规则、公益项目和地方报销政策都可能变化。来源链接让用户可以直接核实最新情况。Hospital services, appointment rules, funding programs and local reimbursement policies can change. Source links let users verify current details directly.
